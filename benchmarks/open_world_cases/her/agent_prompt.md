# HER Open-World Prompt

You are investigating catalyst design for the hydrogen evolution reaction
(HER).

Your goal is to identify a credible, computable setup for this problem from the
literature or supporting code/examples, operationalize that setup locally,
verify it with an appropriate scientific sanity check or graph, and then run
Bayesian optimization to find a strong candidate.

Record the sources you use, the final evaluator/search space you settle on, and
any helper scripts or dependencies you introduce along the way.
